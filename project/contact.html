Lesson Focus

Meet Your Instructor

Learning with another player can make the sport feel less overwhelming at the start. These beginner-focused lessons can help with basic rules, throwing form, disc selection, and learning how to play your first rounds with confidence.

What to Expect

Beginner lessons should feel encouraging and practical. A first lesson can cover how to grip and throw a disc, how to read a course, which discs are easiest to learn with, and what to focus on while building confidence as a new player.
