What Is Disc Golf?

Disc golf is a sport where players throw flying discs from a tee area toward a metal basket, trying to complete each hole in the fewest throws possible. This site introduces the basics of the game, the types of discs players use, and some of the top players in the sport today.

Where to Find a Place to Play

One reason disc golf is so easy to try is that many courses are located in public parks, and a lot of them are free to play. Compared to many other sports, you do not need expensive equipment, reserved court time, or a large group to get started. With just a disc and a nearby course, it is easy for beginners to play a round on their own schedule. Check UDisc for courses near you.

Basic Rules

Players begin each hole at the tee area and throw toward the basket, counting every throw until the disc comes to rest in the target. The player who is farthest from the basket throws next, and the goal is to finish the hole in the fewest throws. A round score is the total number of throws used across all holes, so lower scores are better.
