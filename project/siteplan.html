What in the World is Disc Golf?

Site Name

What in the World is Disc Golf?

This name was chosen because many people have heard of disc golf, but fewer understand what the sport involves or how much it has grown. The title feels inviting, curious, and educational, which matches the goal of introducing new visitors to the sport.

Logo plan: Use a disc golf basket combined with a question mark to match the site's curious, introductory theme.

Site Purpose

The site will answer the main question, "What is disc golf?" by providing resources about the sport's history, current events, professional leaderboards, and information about disc types and flight characteristics.

Target Audience

The target audience is anybody interested in learning more about disc golf or becoming more involved in the sport.

Scenarios

  • How did disc golf become a sport?
  • How many people play disc golf in the United States?
